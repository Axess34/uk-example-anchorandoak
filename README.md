# The Anchor & Oak (UK example)

Fictional example website made by UK Local (https://uklocal.online/) to show what a local business site can look like. The business, address details, phone numbers, prices and reviews are placeholders (UK numbers from Ofcom drama ranges, AU numbers from ACMA fictional ranges).

Live: https://anchorandoak.uklocal.online/
